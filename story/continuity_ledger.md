### Game 2 — "The Charity" (Chapter 3)
**Rules:**
1. Nobody can take your tokens.
2. You may give tokens to anyone in this room. Gifts are final.
3. When time runs out, the poorest player dies. If several tie for poorest, they all die.
4. Zero tokens, and you're not a player anymore.

**Setup:** a white room, a 10-minute limit, and a screen showing only PLAYERS. Momo says "Momo just watches!", which plants the clue that Momo is not a player.

**Loophole:** give every token to Momo. Momo is in the room but is not a player, so there are 0 players and nobody is the poorest.

**What happened:**
1. Everyone except Riku gave Momo all their tokens, so PLAYERS showed 1.
2. With 3 seconds left, Riku gave 1 token to Saki, so PLAYERS showed 2.
3. Saki could not give it to Momo, because that would kill Riku.

**Final game state:**
- **Riku:** 69 tokens. Survives.
- **Saki:** 1 token. Poorest. Executed: lights out, then she is gone and her token lies on the floor.
- **Everyone else:** 0 tokens. Not players.
- **Momo:** holds the rest. The approved text does not state an exact total.

**Survivors:** 19.

## 2. GAME 01 — THE GLASS AUCTION: exact rules

**CONFIRMED CANON (verbatim):**

> Rule 01: The highest unique bid wins the key.
> Rule 02: All spent tokens are permanently lost.
> Rule 03: If two or more players submit the same highest bid, every player tied at that amount is eliminated.
> Rule 04: If the highest bid is not unique, the next-highest unique bid wins.
> Rule 05: If all players leave all tokens unspent when time expires, everyone is executed.

**Parameters:** Players: 20 · Tokens: 100 each · Time limit: 10 minutes · Objective: Obtain the exit key.

**Game state at the end of Chapter 1:** 20 players, 100 tokens each, timer at 09:55. No bids, eliminations or deaths are recorded.

## 3. Unresolved mechanics that affect a valid outcome

No game-state table can be built until the items marked ★ are decided.

**★ A. How Rule 03 interacts with Rule 04 (single pass or cascade).** Example bids: A=60, B=60, C=45, D=45, E=30.
- **Reading 1 (single pass, PROVISIONAL):**
  - Rule 03 removes only the top tie, so A and B are eliminated.
  - Rule 04 skips 45 because it isn't unique.
  - E wins with 30. C and D are not eliminated.
- **Reading 2 (cascade):**
  - After A and B are removed, 45 becomes "the highest bid" and is tied, so C and D are eliminated too.
  - E wins.

Both readings produce the same winner, but they eliminate different numbers of players. This is the bible's question on simultaneous versus sequential tie levels.

**★ B. Submission format:**
- Are bids hidden or public?
- Is there one sealed bid each, or can bids be raised or changed during the 10 minutes?
- Is the result computed only when time expires, or can the game end early?
- How do the token containers work physically?

**★ C. What "eliminated" means.** Does it mean removed from the game, or killed? Rule 03 says "eliminated" and Rule 05 says "executed". The bible forbids treating them as the same without an explicit rule.

**★ D. What the exit key does:**
- Does it let one person out, or every survivor?
- What does it open? The large metal door is not confirmed as the exit.

**★ E. Communication and token transfers.** May players talk, coordinate, or give tokens to each other?
- This is a possible exploit *[audit finding]*. If transfers are allowed, pooled tokens could let one player bid above 100 and guarantee the unique highest bid.

**F. Zero bids.** Are they allowed? Does a bid of 0 count as "unspent" under Rule 05?

**G. Bid format.** Must bids be whole numbers? Is there a minimum? Is the maximum 100? What happens if a player declares more than they hold?

**H. Scope of Rule 02.** Is a bid "spent" the moment it is submitted, by every bidder, or does only the winner pay?

**I. Token persistence.** Do tokens carry into later games? "Permanently lost" hints that they matter later, but this is not confirmed.
- If tokens have no future value, bidding high costs nothing. That changes all rational play.

**J. No unique bid at all.** If every bid is matched by another, what happens? Is everyone tied at the top eliminated, and does anyone win?

**K. Threshold for Rule 05.** Read literally, one player spending one token avoids mass execution. Please confirm this is intended.

**L. Forbidden actions.** What if a player breaks the glass, steals another player's tokens, uses violence, or refuses to take part?

**CONTINUITY CONFLICT *[audit finding]*: birthdays vs. same grade.**
- In Japan, a school grade contains students born from 2 April of one year to 1 April of the next.
- Riku (2006-11-14), Haruto (2007-01-25) and Gō (2007-02-20) fall in the cohort born April 2006–April 2007.
- Ren (2007-04-12) and Yui (2007-06-03) fall in the next cohort, one grade below.
- So under standard rules, these five could not all be third-years in Class 3-B in the same year. The displayed dates are canon, so this needs your decision. Options:
  1. Adjust the dates.
  2. Accept the difference with an in-story reason.
  3. Leave it unexplained.
- This also bears on the story's year. If Ren's and Yui's cohort is correct, the year would be the 2025 school year; if the others' cohort is correct, the 2024 school year.
