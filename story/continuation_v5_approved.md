## Approved answers to Glass Auction mechanics
- **Bids are spent:** every bid is spent; losing bidders are not otherwise punished.
- **The key:** it opens the door for everyone and leads to Game 2.
- **Zero bids:** a player may bid 0 while others bid.
- **Secrecy:** bids are secret and final.
- **Tokens:** they carry over between games.
- **Still UNRESOLVED:** Q3 (no unique bid, but not everyone bid 0) and Q6 (whether "eliminated" means "executed").

## New character
**Nishida Saki**, seat 14, is the ninth named student. The quietest girl in the class. She dies in Game 2.

## Series rule for games (author's direction)
1. **A loophole in every game.** Each game's own rules hide a way for everyone to survive. Ren finds it and fights to save everyone.
2. **People break it.** Greed, fear, betrayal, panic or sacrifice break the loophole. Only a few games end with everyone alive (Game 1 is one); from Game 2 on, most games kill.
3. **The answer is hard.** At least two obvious answers fail on the page first. The real answer combines two rules or turns on one exact word in a rule, never a new rule.
4. **The answer is clear.** One step per sentence, a short proof at the moment of the answer, and every number shown is exact.
5. **The answer is fair.** Its clue is visible before Ren explains it, and the clue for a betrayal is planted before it happens.

## Game 2: The Charity (approved rules)
A white room. A screen shows one number, PLAYERS: 20. Momo sits in the middle of the room, swinging its legs. Ten minutes.
